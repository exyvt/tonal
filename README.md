# Tonal

A premium, user-centric third-party music management and Last.fm client for iOS, built with SwiftUI. Designed to make music tracking, stats, and discovery actually fun.

## Features

- **Stats & Facts**: Dive into comprehensive listening analytics, fun facts, milestones, and your full music profile.
- **Scrobble History**: Browse your last 1,000 scrobbles with detailed list and chart views.
- **Explore & Discover**: Search the Last.fm database to view any artist, album, or track, complete with rich global stats and top rankings.
- **Scrobble on the Go**: Manually scrobble individual tracks or whole albums directly from the app.
- **Deep Customization**: Personalize your experience with custom accent colors, font choices, theme modes, and card styles.
- **Top Metrics**: Instantly view your top artists, albums, and tracks across various timeframes.
- **Adaptive UI**: Built with a gorgeous fluid glass SwiftUI interface featuring seamless light and dark mode support (with system theme switching).

## Requirements

- **iOS Version**: iOS 17.0 or later.
- **Installation**: Requires a computer (Mac or Windows or even Linux) along with a sideloading tool like **Sideloadly** or **3uTools** to install the `.ipa` file.

## About

Created with passion by an independent hobbyist music and tech enthusiast. *Tonal* is not officially affiliated with Last.fm.
